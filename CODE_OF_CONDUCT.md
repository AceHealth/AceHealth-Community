# Community guidelines

Help make Ace a welcoming space for people with different backgrounds, abilities, goals, and experience levels.

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and behavior without attacking people.
- Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's personal or health information.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, spam, or post sexual content.
- Do not promote unsafe practices or present community replies as professional medical advice.
- Keep discussions relevant to Ace and its community.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, or restrict participation when these guidelines are violated. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Use GitHub's **Report content** option to flag abuse privately to GitHub. For moderation by the Ace team, contact a repository maintainer through a private channel they list on their GitHub profile. Do not repeat private details in a public report. If no private channel is listed, use GitHub's reporting tools.
