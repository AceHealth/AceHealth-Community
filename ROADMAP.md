# Ace roadmap

The public roadmap follows feature requests in this repository. Each request describes a user problem and can collect feedback before the team commits to building it.

| Stage | Meaning | View |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under consideration | Gathering feedback; no commitment to build | [View ideas](https://github.com/AceHealth/AceHealth-Community/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20is%3Aopen%20label%3A%22type%3A%20feature%22%20label%3A%22status%3A%20under%20consideration%22) |
| Planned | Accepted for future work; no delivery date promised | [View planned](https://github.com/AceHealth/AceHealth-Community/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20is%3Aopen%20label%3A%22type%3A%20feature%22%20label%3A%22status%3A%20planned%22) |
| In progress | Actively being worked on | [View in progress](https://github.com/AceHealth/AceHealth-Community/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20is%3Aopen%20label%3A%22type%3A%20feature%22%20label%3A%22status%3A%20in%20progress%22) |
| Shipped | Available in a published release | [View shipped](https://github.com/AceHealth/AceHealth-Community/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20label%3A%22type%3A%20feature%22%20label%3A%22status%3A%20shipped%22) |

No feature commitments have been published here yet. Empty lists mean there are no public items in that stage.

[Suggest a feature](https://github.com/AceHealth/AceHealth-Community/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml) or add a 👍 to an existing request. Reactions help the team understand interest; they do not guarantee a delivery date. Plans can change, and dates are only announced when the team is ready to commit.

Shipped items link to their [release notes](https://github.com/AceHealth/AceHealth-Community/releases). See the [maintainer guide](docs/MAINTAINERS.md) for triage and publishing steps.
